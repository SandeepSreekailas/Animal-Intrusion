Empowering Farmers with Technology

Traditional farming faces distinct challenges from wildlife intrusion, leading to significant crop loss and financial strain. AnimalGuard bridges the gap between traditional agriculture and modern security.

Our system uses Computer Vision and Deep Learning to create a virtual fence around your property. It distinguishes between harmless movement and actual threats, sending real-time alerts so you can take action immediately.

How AnimalGuard Works

1

Smart Detection

Cameras installed around your farm continuously scan for movement using our AI models, filtering out wind and shadows.

2

Instant Analysis

When an animal is spotted, the system identifies the species (Elephant, Boar, etc.) in milliseconds.

3

Real-Time Alerts

You receive an instant notification on your dashboard and via email with a snapshot of the intrusion.
